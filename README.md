# Hot Dog Stand – The Game

**Q:** What happens when the electrician installs a [21-gang light switch](https://www.reddit.com/r/electricians/comments/1vrpfw5/21_gang/) in your hot dog restaurant, mixes everything up with 3-way circuits, doesn't label anything, and then some random podcast host decides to vibe-code a game from it?

**A:** A randomized puzzle game about opening a restaurant with 21 unlabeled light switches!

Try your skill at finding the right combination of switch flips to turn on all the lights in the hot dog stand! Each level starts with a new random circuit and adds up to 6 lights to increase difficulty. A switch may operate no lights, a single light, or multiple lights at once. Multiple switches on a circuit will act like hidden 3-way or multi-way wiring.

Good luck!
